Height-conditioned core geometry

Explicit stair plans: two landings and two flights, followed by a configurable lift schedule at higher storeys. Both the 19–30 and 31–60 storey bands use two interlocking scissor routes in one enclosure, each with an intermediate landing at +1.5 m. Four lifts fill the bank beside the stairs; its plan includes developed lane profiles. The low-rise band has stairs only. A 6–10-storey core reserves 5 × 5 m on the one-metre voxel grid, rather than the previous 6 × 8 m block.

Solid geometry uses metric dimensions; the pale outer band shows grid rounding. Height bands and lift counts are research assumptions, not a claim of building-code compliance or lift traffic capacity. These plans define service-space reservations for an ML environment.
